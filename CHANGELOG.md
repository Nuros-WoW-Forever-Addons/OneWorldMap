## [0.0.1] - 2026-10-11
### Added
- **Initial Addon Scaffolding**: Setup `OneWorldMap.toc` with multi-client interface targets (16001, 11200, 20400, 30300, 110000) and `OneWorldMapDB` SavedVariables.
- **Hybrid Shell Frame Architecture (`Shell.lua`)**: Clean custom outer frame shell encapsulating `WorldMapFrame`, supporting dragging, window repositioning, and movement fade.
- **DoubleWide Quest Log (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Dual-pane quest layout decoupling `QuestMapFrame.DetailsFrame` to sit side-by-side with `QuestMapFrame.QuestsFrame`, eliminating the need to click back and forth.
- **Left Zone Metadata Sidebar (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Collapsible side drawer displaying zone level ranges, Herbalism/Mining/Fishing gathering thresholds, and local dungeon brackets with a `[ < ]` / `[ > ]` toggle.
- **Throttled Coordinates Bar (`Coordinates.lua`)**: 10 Hz ticker bar showing `Player: XX.X, YY.Y | Cursor: XX.X, YY.Y` with canvas bounds validation and dungeon nil-guards.
- **Static Zone Database (`Data/ZoneData.lua`)**: Baseline database mapping `uiMapID` to level ranges, gathering thresholds, and dungeon recommendations.
- **Fog-of-War Exploration Reveal (`MapReveal.lua`, `Data/FogDataForever.lua`, `Data/FogDataStandard.lua`)**: Exploration overlay reveal module with atmospheric Darkened / Shaded Fog Tint for modern Retail/Classic engine (11.x, Interface 16001). Unexplored terrain tiles are desaturated (`SetDesaturation(1)`) and tinted with a cool charcoal/slate shadow (default `{r=0.38, g=0.38, b=0.45}` at `0.65` opacity) beneath native pins and quest objective blobs, leaving roads, rivers, and topography visible while distinctly shrouding unexplored regions in Fog of War. Explored tiles are restored to full brightness and saturation. Includes preset support (`charcoal`, `dark`, `blue`, `sepia`, `light`) and custom RGB tinting.
- **Graphical Options Panel (`OptionsUI.lua`)**: Comprehensive configuration panel available as both an independent floating dialog (`/owm`, ESC-closeable, movable) and an integrated Blizzard settings category (`Game Menu -> Options -> AddOns -> OneWorldMap`). Features dual-parenting, a "Pop Out Independent Window" button to pop out from the game menu, real-time interactive checkboxes, steppers, preset buttons, and live settings synchronization across Map Window, DoubleWide Quest Log, Zone Metadata Sidebar, Live Coordinates, Movement Ergonomics, and Fog-of-War.
- **Slash Command Handler (`Core.lua`)**: Registered `/oneworldmap`, `/owm`, `/onemap`, and `/om` commands. Typing `/owm` without arguments directly opens the Options Panel.

### Fixed
- **Coordinate Redundancy & Default Suppression (`Coordinates.lua`)**: Added deep recursive detection and suppression of default unstyled coordinates (stacked cursor/player readout at bottom-left), ensuring only the preferred high-contrast black background pill box is shown.
- **DoubleWide Quest Persistence (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Intercepted `QuestMapFrame.QuestsFrame.ScrollFrame.Hide` (the actual container holding quest headers and titles) to ensure the quest list stays visible side-by-side with details.
- **Scrollbar Anchoring & Bounds (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Explicitly anchored and constrained `QuestMapDetailsScrollFrame.ScrollBar` within `DetailsFrame` bounds (`-8, -48` and `-8, 28`), preventing the scrollbar from floating off the panel.
- **Engine-Accurate Column Sizing (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Adjusted column widths to match the native XML geometry (330 px list column + 308 px detail column) with safe base width caching.
- **Quest List Scrollbar Separation (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Constrained `QuestScrollFrame` with a 24 px right margin and explicitly anchored `QuestScrollFrame.ScrollBar` at `TOPRIGHT, questsFrame, -4, -31` and `BOTTOMRIGHT, questsFrame, -4, 4`, preventing any overlap with the Details pane.
- **Canvas Lock & Left Edge Anchor (`DoubleWide.lua`, `Shell.lua`)**: Locked `WorldMapFrame.ScrollContainer` to `TOPLEFT, WorldMapFrame, 4, -68` with a fixed width, and anchored `WorldMapFrame` to its `TOPLEFT` screen position so that expanding width only grows outward to the right, preventing the map canvas from shifting and eliminating wooden border gaps.
- **Tracking Cog Repositioning (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Re-anchored `WorldMapFrame.WorldMapTrackingOptionsButton` ("Show Quest Objectives" cog) to `LEFT, WorldMapFrame.NavBar, RIGHT, 6, -1`, keeping it on the navigation header and preventing it from overlapping the quest counter.
- **Clean Quest List Collapse (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Hooked `WorldMapFrame.SetQuestLogPanelShown` and `QuestMapFrame` visibility so that collapsing the quest list shrinks `WorldMapFrame` to align flush against the right edge of the map canvas without leaving a blank grey void.
- **Full-Height Quest Panels (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Re-anchored `QuestMapFrame` from `y = -25` down to `y = 3`, eliminating the top gap and extending both Quest List and Quest Details fully from the top border to the bottom border.
- **Authentic Bronze Frame (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Replaced the grey metal dialog box border with the authentic Blizzard bronze `NineSlice` border (`Dialog` layout) with warm bronze tint and dark fill matching `WorldMapFrame`.
- **Interior Title (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Removed the protruding `UI-DialogBox-Header` ribbon plate; placed a clean `"Zone Details"` gold title inside the frame at the top above the content table.
- **290 px Panel Width & SavedVariables Migration (`Sidebar.lua`, `Core.lua`)**: Increased sidebar default width to 290 px (inner content width 244 px) and added automatic SavedVariables migration to prevent old 180 px settings from clipping content, ensuring all item names and values display in full without truncation.
- **Centered Profession Headers (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Centered profession names (`Herbalism`, `Mining`, `Skinning`, `Fishing`, `Dungeons`, `<Zone Name>`) inside the grey bevel buttons and removed `-`/`+` prefixes.
- **Gold Triangle Arrow Collapse Button (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Replaced the text button with a gold/yellow triangle arrow button at the inner bottom-left corner of the map canvas, pointing RIGHT (`>`) to collapse the drawer and pointing LEFT (`<`) to expand.
- **Fish Species Database (`Data/ZoneData.lua`, `Sidebar.lua`)**: Cataloged local fish species and skill requirements for all Classic zones.
- **Coordinates De-duplication (`Sidebar.lua`, `Coordinates.lua`)**: Kept coordinates exclusively in the high-contrast black pill box on the map canvas.
- **Authentic SpellBook Arrow Toggle Button (`Sidebar.lua`)**: Updated inner bottom-left sidebar toggle to use 26x26 `UI-SpellBookIcon-NextPage-Up/Down` and `UI-SpellBookIcon-PrevPage-Up/Down` textures, eliminating black background square artifacts and properly pointing RIGHT to collapse and LEFT to expand.
- **Quest List & Details Geometry & Scrollbar Alignment (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Restored native 330 px column width for `QuestsFrame`, locked the Quest List scrollbar to the far right margin so it never overlaps quest checkmarks or header collapse icons, and removed manual cog overrides so the options button naturally clears `Quests: 22/40`.
- **DetailsFrame Native Width (308 px) & Sizing (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Sized `DetailsFrame` to its authentic 308 px width, eliminating the outer frame excess gap on the right.
- **Symmetrical 22 px Gutter Architecture (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Configured both `QuestsFrame` and `DetailsFrame` to dual 330 px columns, each with an identical 22 px scrollbar gutter along its right edge backed by a dark track background (`ScrollGutterBg`, `0.06, 0.06, 0.07, 0.95`). Centered `MinimalScrollBar` in both gutters with an identical 8 px offset, achieving 100% visual, dimensional, and behavioral symmetry between the Quest List and Quest Details scrollbars.
- **Additive Dual 330 px Panel Architecture (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Restored the exact default Blizzard interface layout where the Quest List panel is 330 px wide (308 px content + 22 px gutter), preserving all quest titles and checkboxes without truncation. When Quest Details is displayed, an identically dimensioned 330 px Details panel is added directly to the right (`660 px` total quest area, expanding WorldMapFrame additively by `+330 px`).
- **Symmetrical 22 px Slate-Gray Gutters & Centered Scrollbars (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Created identical 22 px wide slate-gray scrollbar track backgrounds (`ScrollGutterBg`, `0.18, 0.18, 0.20, 0.95`) on both the Quest List and Details panels. Centered both scrollbars identically at `x = 7` with uniform top/bottom limits (`y = 2` to `y = -2`, stopping at `y = 23` above the bottom border).
- **Parchment Background Isolation (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Constrained `DetailsFrame.Bg` to the 308 px content area (`-22 px` on the right) so the parchment texture stops before the scrollbar gutter and never bleeds behind the scrollbar.
- **Details Bottom Action Buttons Constrained (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Constrained `AbandonButton`, `ShareButton`, and `TrackButton` to 304 px within the 308 px content area, keeping the right 22 px gutter clear for the scrollbar and down arrow.
- **Combat Taint & Protected Call Elimination (`DoubleWide.lua`, `Coordinates.lua`)**: Removed raw `.Hide` method overrides on `QuestsFrame` and `QuestScrollFrame` which tainted the execution stack during combat and blocked protected calls (`Button:SetPassThroughButtons()`). Replaced with non-tainting `hooksecurefunc` listeners. Excluded `WorldMapFrame.ScrollContainer` from recursive coordinate scans to prevent touching map pin widgets.
- **Combat Details Overlay Elimination & Pre-Anchoring (`DoubleWide.lua`)**: Pre-anchored `DetailsFrame` permanently side-by-side at `x = 330` on addon initialization, preventing it from ever falling back to Blizzard's default overlapping position. Removed combat lockdown restriction from `ApplyDoubleWideLayout()`, allowing the frame to expand and position side-by-side seamlessly and immediately during combat without overlaying or waiting for combat to end.
- **Complete Zone, Capital City & Dungeon Database (`Data/ZoneData.lua`, `Sidebar.lua`)**: Expanded `Data/ZoneData.lua` from 14 starter zones to all 45+ Classic zones, all 6 Capital Cities (Stormwind City `1453` with canal Fishing and The Stockade 22–30, Orgrimmar `1454` with Fishing and Ragefire Chasm 13–18, Ironforge `1455`, Thunder Bluff `1456`, Darnassus `1457`, Undercity `1458`), and WoW Forever custom zones (`Mount Hyjal`, `Zephras Isle`, `Riverglades`, `Shen'dralas`). Fixed UIMapID mappings for Loch Modan (`1432`), Redridge Mountains (`1433`), and Teldrassil (`1438`), and updated `Sidebar.lua` to display `"Zone Type: Capital City"` for major cities.
